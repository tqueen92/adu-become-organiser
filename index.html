Your role

As an organiser, you help shape what our community learns. Our classic format is the panel discussion, run by 1–2 organisers. Together, you:

  • Choose the topic and the angle
  • Find speakers and a moderator, with help from our domain advisors
  • Prepare questions and run prep calls with the speakers
  • Make the event slides, run the event day and write the takeaways

In 2027 we're also trying out new formats next to our panels, starting with talks. It's a chance to experiment and shape something new with us.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form. We read every answer with care. We run ADU in our free time, next to busy jobs, so replies can take a while, especially around holidays. Thanks for your patience! 🙏
  2. Have a chat. If your profile fits one of our upcoming topics, an organiser who works on similar topics will reach out for a short, relaxed conversation.
  3. Join a trial. You'll organise one event together with 1–2 of our organisers. It's a chance for you to see if you enjoy it, and for us to see if it's a good fit. No commitment before that.
  4. Welcome to the team! 🎉

Why we ask these questions

To find the right speakers, you need to know the topic well: well enough to spot a strong speaker, ask good questions, and make the session valuable for everyone in the room.

General topics

Broad experience is enough.

e.g. Exploring the Data PM Path

In-depth topics

Need deep knowledge. A data scientist may find it hard to judge AI engineering speakers, and the other way around.

e.g. Causal Inference, Agentic AI for Self-Service Analytics, Agentic Guardrails in Engineering

The questions in the form help us match you with the topics where you'll shine most. They take about 10 minutes.
